Across the brand (n=38,092), Overall Experience was essentially flat to slightly down versus last month (-0.02) and flat versus this month last year, but still below the longer-term trend (last 12 months YoY -0.18). The team's results reflect stability month-to-month driven by strong kid-facing execution, while adult-facing and dining/cleanliness fundamentals remain the main constraints to broader improvement.

Child Experience remains the brand's anchor, improving slightly versus last month (+0.1) and holding at a high level; the team's entertainment additions also continue to show strength year-over-year (Trampoline +0.1 YoY; Superhero Playground +0.3 YoY). Games & Rides stayed stable with low average downtime (~1.1 games down per 100), supporting consistent play. **Data-quality note:** Chuck E. visibility fields report 100% across mutually exclusive options, indicating a measurement/data-quality issue rather than a true read on character exposure.

Where the team is losing ground year-over-year is primarily in the adult/parent experience ecosystem: Adult Experience is down (-0.1 YoY this month; -0.2 over last 3/12 months) and continues to trail Child Experience by roughly 0.8 points. Dining-related attributes are mostly flat month-to-month but show softening over longer horizons (Dining Experience, Taste, Accuracy, Quantity, and Speed all down vs last year in last-3/12-month comparisons), with Food Service Speed standing out as both low-rated and persistently pressured.

Regionally, the Southeast is the primary positive contributor, running about +0.1 above the brand on Overall Experience with broad-based strengths in cleanliness and dining execution. The Northeast is roughly in-line with the brand overall, with solid value and service pockets but more mixed bathroom cleanliness. The Western region is the main drag at ~0.1 below brand Overall Experience and under-indexes across multiple drivers (Adult Experience, Games & Rides, Dining, Speed, Cleanliness, and Kid Check). Several higher-sample Western districts (notably Octavio Castaneda, Claudia Rivas, Patty Cedillo, and Yuri Shafran) sit ~0.2-0.3 below brand Overall Experience, collectively exerting more downward pressure on the brand than any single district elsewhere.

On bright spots, Value for the Money continues to improve (up vs last month and +0.4 YoY), and Employee Service edged up MoM (+0.1) with fewer guests reporting unpleasant employee encounters (6% vs 7%). Franchise results are materially above brand averages across nearly all attributes, but the sample is comparatively small (n=449), so it should be treated as directional rather than fully comparable to corporate scale.

## What Made Overall Scores Go Up


### Strong child fun foundation remains the team's core strength

Brand-level Child Experience is holding at a high level and modestly improved vs last month (+0.1), helping stabilize Overall Experience despite softness in several supporting drivers year-over-year.

Regionally, Southeast and Northeast sit at/near the brand average for Child Experience, while Western trends slightly lower, creating mild drag on the brand's family fun perception. Trampoline and Superhero Playground continue to be standout kid-facing elements, with YoY gains (Trampoline +0.1; Superhero Playground +0.3) reinforcing the entertainment proposition where present.

**Data-quality note:** Chuck E. activity visibility fields show 100% across options, indicating a data quality issue rather than a true read on character engagement.

### Operational game uptime remains a consistent positive baseline

Games & Rides remains steady vs last month (flat) with low average inoperable games (~1.1 per 100) supporting a reliable play experience. Southeast is slightly stronger on uptime (closer to 1.0/100) versus Western (closer to 1.2/100), aligning with Western's slightly lower Games & Rides ratings and helping explain the region's overall underperformance vs brand average.

### Employee Service is stable-to-improving vs prior month with fewer negative encounters

Employee Service ticked up vs last month (+0.1) and the share reporting an unpleasant encounter improved (6% vs 7%). District variability matters: top-performing districts (e.g., Bill Pressel, Jim Hamblen, Susan Jensen, Tony Reynolds) run materially above brand average on service (+0.2 to +0.4), helping lift their Overall Experience above brand.

Conversely, several Western districts (e.g., Patty Cedillo) sit notably below brand average on Employee Service (about -0.5), acting as a consistent headwind.

### Perceived value continues a meaningful YoY improvement

Value for the Money improved vs last month (+0.1) and is up strongly YoY (+0.4), a notable bright spot against broader 12-month declines in several experience attributes. Northeast and Western are roughly in-line with brand on value, while select districts (e.g., Troy Watring and Adam Shafran) are meaningfully above brand average (~+0.3), supporting stronger overall performance in those areas.

### Cleanliness holds steady with pockets of outperformance supporting trust

Brand Cleanliness is flat vs last month and remains a key stabilizer; Southeast trends slightly above brand average (+0.1) while Western trends below (-0.2). High-performing districts (e.g., Jim Hamblen, Frank Ramirez) sit notably above brand average on both overall and bathroom cleanliness (often +0.3 to +0.6), contributing to their higher Overall Experience.

## What Made Overall Scores Go Down


### Overall Experience is essentially flat MoM but still below longer-term brand trend

Overall Experience dipped slightly vs last month (-0.02) and remains down vs the last 12-month benchmark (12-month YoY -0.18). The pattern suggests the team is maintaining month-to-month stability but not fully recovering prior-year levels across core drivers like Adult Experience, Dining Experience, and Cleanliness which show multi-month YoY softness.

### Western region is the primary drag on brand performance across multiple drivers

Western Overall Experience runs ~0.1 below brand average, with consistent underperformance in Adult Experience (~-0.2), Games & Rides (~-0.2), Dining (~-0.1), Food Service Speed (~-0.2), Cleanliness (~-0.2), and Kid Check (~-0.1). Several larger-sample Western districts (e.g., Octavio Castaneda, Claudia Rivas, Patty Cedillo, Yuri Shafran) are materially below brand average in Overall Experience (roughly -0.2 to -0.3), collectively pulling down brand results more than any single Northeast/Southeast district.

### Adult/Parent experience continues to lag the child experience and is down YoY

Adult Experience is significantly below Child Experience (about -0.8 points gap) and declined vs last year (-0.1 this month; -0.2 over last 3/12 months). This gap is widest in Western (Adult Experience ~-0.2 vs brand), indicating that even where kids are having fun, the team's adult-facing comfort/convenience elements (speed, cleanliness consistency, perceived order flow) are not keeping pace.

### Food Service Speed remains one of the lowest-rated core attributes and soft YoY

Food Service Speed is flat vs last month but remains among the lowest core operational scores and is down vs last year over 3/12-month views (-0.1). Western is the most challenged (about -0.2 vs brand), with multiple districts notably below brand average (e.g., Patty Cedillo and Claudia Rivas at ~-0.6; Sheila Kobe and Joe Risnear in Southeast at ~-0.4). This recurring friction likely contributes to Adult Experience softness and suppresses Overall Dining Experience.

### Bathroom cleanliness remains a consistent vulnerability vs other cleanliness measures

Bathroom Cleanliness sits ~0.3 below overall Cleanliness at the brand level and is down YoY (-0.1). Western and several Northeast districts show the weakest bathroom ratings (often -0.2 to -0.4 vs brand), making restrooms a repeat 'trust' gap that can disproportionately affect adult satisfaction and revisit intent.

## What to Focus on Most


### Child Experience (fun delivery)

The team's strongest and most protective driver. Child Experience is high and slightly improved vs last month, keeping Overall Experience stable even as adult/dining metrics soften. YoY declines over 3/12 months indicate incremental erosion, but this remains the brand's anchor metric.

### Employee Service (friendliness + issue resolution)

Service rose slightly MoM and negative encounter rates improved, suggesting the team's frontline execution is stabilizing. District-level service dispersion is meaningful and is a key differentiator between top and bottom Overall Experience districts.

### Cleanliness & Bathroom Cleanliness (environmental trust)

Cleanliness is steady MoM but down YoY; bathrooms remain the more fragile sub-driver. Western's consistent under-indexing on these measures appears to be a repeat contributor to its lower Adult Experience and overall brand drag.

### Food Service Speed (operational friction)

Speed is one of the lowest-rated core attributes and shows persistent weakness, particularly in Western and a few Southeast districts. Where speed is depressed, Dining Experience and Adult Experience tend to run below brand average.

### Games & Rides (availability + reliability)

Ratings are stable MoM and supported by low reported downtime; however, Western's slightly higher inoperable count aligns with lower Games & Rides ratings. This remains a key experiential pillar but is not the primary source of month-to-month movement this period.

### Value for the Money (price-value equation)

Value continues to improve YoY and modestly MoM, helping offset declines elsewhere. While still the lowest absolute-rated attribute, its directionality is a relative bright spot and contributes positively to overall sentiment where districts are above brand average.
